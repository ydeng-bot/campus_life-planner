About

Campus Life Planner helps students plan tasks and events, track how long each one takes, and stay under a weekly time cap. Add tasks with a due date, duration and tag, search them with regular expressions, and see your workload on the dashboard.

Built by Yom Deng Garang as a vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript project.
